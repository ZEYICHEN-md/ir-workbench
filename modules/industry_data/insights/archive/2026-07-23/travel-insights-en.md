## Weekly

### Risk

#### Domestic hotel RevPAR keeps softening

Latest week with data (7/5–7/11): domestic hotel RevPAR **-6.5%** YoY, occupancy **-4.8%**, ADR **-1.8%**; prior week (6/28–7/4) RevPAR was also **-5.3%**. Both occupancy and ADR were negative, so the RevPAR decline was not driven by a single component alone.

*Refs: weekly.hotelRevPAR@7/5-7/11; weekly.hotelOccupancy@7/5-7/11; weekly.hotelADR@7/5-7/11; weekly.hotelRevPAR@6/28-7/4*

#### Airfares and flights both weak

7/5–7/11 airfares **-8.0%** YoY and passenger flights **-4.0%**; fares have been negative for three weeks since 6/21–6/27, while pax improved to about **-1.0%**. Recent weeks show narrower pax declines alongside still-soft fares and flight volume.

*Refs: weekly.aviationTicket@7/5-7/11; weekly.aviationFlight@7/5-7/11; weekly.aviationPax@7/5-7/11; weekly.aviationTicket@6/21-6/27*

### Outlook

#### Pax decline narrows; summer still TBD

Pax YoY improved from about **-10%** (5/31–6/06) to **-1%** in the latest week. The narrowing is clear, but whether mid/late-July weekly prints can hold still needs confirmation.

*Refs: weekly.aviationPax@5/31-6/06; weekly.aviationPax@7/5-7/11*

## Monthly

### Highlight

#### Rail pax still positive, slowing

Railway pax YoY stayed positive through Jan–Jun; May **+1.9%**, June **+0.4%**. Growth remains positive, but the pace slowed noticeably from May to June.

*Refs: monthly.railway@5月; monthly.railway@6月*

### Risk

#### Domestic air pressured in May–Jun

CAAC domestic pax was **-7.8%** YoY in May and **-7.1%** in June; Big3 June about **-7.8%** (May about -9.3%). Both sources were deeply negative for two straight months, with no clear narrowing in June.

*Refs: monthly.domAviationCAAC@5月; monthly.domAviationCAAC@6月; monthly.domAviationBig3@6月*

#### Intl pax turned weaker in June

Intl route pax (cross-border) was **-2.0%** YoY (CAAC) and about **-0.2%** (Big3) in June, after near-flat/slightly positive May prints. Intl capacity (vs 2019) is still latest at May **-15.0%**; June capacity is not yet disclosed.

*Refs: monthly.intlAviationCAAC@6月; monthly.intlAviationBig3@6月; monthly.intlCapacity@5月*

### Outlook

#### Domestic hotel June and summer months are key gaps

Domestic hotel RevPAR is still latest at May **+0.8%**; domestic air has been deeply negative for two months and intl pax weakened in June. June domestic hotel RevPAR is not yet published and is the next priority gap to check.

*Refs: monthly.hotelRevPAR@5月; monthly.domAviationCAAC@6月; monthly.intlAviationCAAC@6月*

## Quarterly

### Highlight

#### Intl routes strong in Q1

Q1 intl air pax about **+10.3%–+12.8%** YoY (CAAC / Big3); capacity vs 2019 still about **-9.6%**. Pax turned positive YoY while capacity vs 2019 remained below 2019 — recovery timing differed across the two sides.

*Refs: quarterly.q1.intlAviationCAAC; quarterly.q1.intlAviationBig3; quarterly.q1.intlCapacity*

#### Rail and domestic hotels positive in Q1

Q1 railway pax about **+5.7%**; domestic hotel RevPAR about **+4.8%** (ADR about +3.8%). Both were positive, with domestic hotel RevPAR more ADR-led.

*Refs: quarterly.q1.railway; quarterly.q1.hotelRevPAR; quarterly.q1.hotelADR*

### Risk

#### Q2 metrics clearly softer than Q1

Q2 (Apr–Jun monthly avg) domestic air (CAAC) about **-5.2%**, below Q1 about **+5.5%**; railway pax about **+4.3%**, still positive but below Q1. Domestic air’s swing from positive to negative was one of the larger Q1→Q2 moves.

*Refs: quarterly.q2.domAviationCAAC; quarterly.q1.domAviationCAAC; quarterly.q2.railway; quarterly.q1.railway*

### Outlook

#### Can summer reverse a soft Q2 start

On full Q2 averages, domestic air is negative and railway growth is below Q1. Before July–August peak-season data arrive, it is still too early to draw firm conclusions on H2 pacing.

*Refs: quarterly.q2.domAviationCAAC; quarterly.q2.railway*
