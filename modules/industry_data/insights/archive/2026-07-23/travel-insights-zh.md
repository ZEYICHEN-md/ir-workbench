*引用：weekly.aviationTicket@7/5-7/11；weekly.aviationFlight@7/5-7/11；weekly.aviationPax@7/5-7/11；weekly.aviationTicket@6/21-6/27*

*引用：quarterly.q1.intlAviationCAAC；quarterly.q1.intlAviationBig3；quarterly.q1.intlCapacity*

*引用：quarterly.q1.railway；quarterly.q1.hotelRevPAR；quarterly.q1.hotelADR*
