> Data as of **2026-08-08**; projected from `travel-insights.json` for reuse in briefs and docs.

## Weekly

### Highlight

#### Pax positive four straight weeks

8/2–8/8 domestic air pax was **+4.0%** YoY, a fourth straight positive week, but down from +8.0% the prior week, with flights about **+2.0%**. Summer traffic is still there; YoY momentum has cooled from the late-July high.

*Refs: weekly.aviationPax@7/19-7/25; weekly.aviationPax@7/26-8/1; weekly.aviationPax@8/2-8/8; weekly.aviationFlight@8/2-8/8*

### Risk

#### Volume-up, price-down intact; fares -8%

8/2–8/8 airfares were **-8.0%** YoY, weaker than -7.0% the prior week — stronger pax did not lift yields. Ample capacity and price-sensitive travelers, plus the Aug 5 fuel-surcharge cut, kept inclusive fares under YoY pressure.

*Refs: weekly.aviationTicket@7/26-8/1; weekly.aviationTicket@8/2-8/8; weekly.aviationPax@8/2-8/8*

#### Hotel RevPAR narrowing stalled

8/2–8/8 hotel RevPAR was **-1.5%** YoY, widening from -0.9% the prior week (occupancy **-1.6%**, ADR nearly flat). The three-week narrowing run broke; a turn to positive is still unproven.

*Refs: weekly.hotelRevPAR@7/19-7/25; weekly.hotelRevPAR@7/26-8/1; weekly.hotelRevPAR@8/2-8/8; weekly.hotelOccupancy@8/2-8/8; weekly.hotelADR@8/2-8/8*

### Outlook

#### Can mid/late August re-converge volume and price

Watch whether pax YoY holds, fare declines narrow, and hotel RevPAR resumes tightening. If traffic cools while prices stay weak, summer yield repair has even less room.

*Refs: weekly.aviationPax@8/2-8/8; weekly.aviationTicket@8/2-8/8; weekly.hotelRevPAR@8/2-8/8*

## Monthly

### Risk

#### June domestic air still deeply negative

The latest complete month (June) CAAC domestic pax was **-7.1%** YoY, Big3 about **-7.8%**. Q2 high fuel and capacity cuts were the main drivers; July monthly is still out, while weekly pax has already turned positive — whether that prints at month level is unproven.

*Refs: monthly.domAviationCAAC@6月; monthly.domAviationBig3@6月*

#### Intl capacity gap vs 2019 still wide

June capacity vs 2019 was **-16.3%** YoY; intl pax about **-2.0%**. The shortfall persists; watch intl add-backs and inbound demand conversion.

*Refs: monthly.intlCapacity@6月; monthly.intlAviationCAAC@6月*

June hotels were volume-down, price-stable (occupancy **-2.5%**, ADR **+2.2%**); against that, 8/2–8/8 pax is +4.0% YoY, fares -8.0%, hotel RevPAR -1.5%. July monthly will show whether summer improvement lands at month level.

*Refs: monthly.hotelOccupancy@6月; monthly.hotelADR@6月; weekly.aviationPax@8/2-8/8; weekly.aviationTicket@8/2-8/8; weekly.hotelRevPAR@8/2-8/8*
