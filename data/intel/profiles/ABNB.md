### 2026-08-19 · Airbnb曾下调三个月后的长住房东费以抑制脱媒

原话：「Prior to this point, even if your stay length was one month or five months, we were charging the same fee to the host. What that essentially incentivizes is disintermediation. Once the guest and the host connect, why do they need to keep using Airbnb? They can very simply go offline and pay each other directly. To disincentivize this behavior, we created a new fee curve or a revised fee structure for longer stays. After month three, the fee that was charged to hosts dropped dramatically.」
